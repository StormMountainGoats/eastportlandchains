Why We Exist

Bike security should be strong, flexible, serviceable, and priced fairly.

Many affordable locks compromise on materials or usable length. Many stronger systems are rigid, permanently integrated, or priced far above the cost of their components.

East Portland Chains takes a more practical approach. We combine Pewag chain, dependable locking hardware, and serviceable construction into a system designed to provide serious everyday security without unnecessary markup.
