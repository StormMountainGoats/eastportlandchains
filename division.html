Padlock Compatibility

Use a lock that fits the chain and the way you ride.

The Division is designed for keyed padlocks with an 8 mm or smaller shackle. A hardened-steel, short-shackle padlock with a solid metal body and double-bolted locking mechanism is a strong practical match.

The chain-only configuration lets you use a compatible lock you already own. The chain-and-padlock configuration includes an approved padlock selected from current stock.

Your Lock. Your Choice.

The padlock is separate from the chain on purpose.

A separate padlock gives you more control over the system. You can use compatible hardware you already trust, replace a damaged or lost lock without discarding the chain, and upgrade the locking hardware later.

The chain and padlock can be inspected and replaced separately instead of being permanently integrated into one unit.
